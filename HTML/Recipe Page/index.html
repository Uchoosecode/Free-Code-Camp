My Recepie

This is my favorite recipe

Ingredients

  • Love
  • Peace
  • Happiness
  • Positivity

Instructions

  1. Put 2 cups of love in a bowl
  2. Add 1 liter of peace and 1 liter of happiness
  3. Mix for 5 minutes until all the ingredients are blended together
  4. Sprinkle a tablespoon of positivity then serve
